THE FIVE DOMINIONS OF SHANDALAR

01 / The adventure

One world.
Endless decks.

Shandalar turns deck building into an adventure. Every road leads to a new rival, a better card, or a choice that changes your run.

EXPLORE

Cross a living overworld

Travel through forests, deserts, mountains, cities, and dungeons across a generated isometric realm.

BUILD

Shape your spellbook

Trade in towns, complete quests, and tune multiple decks as your collection grows.

DUEL

Outplay rival wizards

Fight full rules-engine battles, win cards by ante, and hunt the five amulets that guard the plane.
